## Context & Key Findings

**Root cause of most parity failures: TIMING.** The combined mart (`THIRD_PARTY_RECON_DETAIL`, last altered 19:02) is 3+ hours stale vs individual vendor tables (rebuilt 22:25-22:30). When both are fresh, most row-count mismatches disappear.

**Remaining structural issues** (verified by live queries):
- **SentinelOne**: +1-3 rows in combined (stale snapshot) + billing_qty drift of 2k-7k/month (individual added/changed rows after combined was built)
- **Webroot**: `WEBROOT_RECON_DETAIL_APP` now equals `WEBROOT_RECON_DETAIL` — the -1 row gap is purely staleness
- **ESET June**: Same row counts, but 1,110 billing_qty difference — ESET_RECON_DETAIL was refreshed with new billing data after combined was built
- **Proofpoint**: +2 billing_qty in Jan-Apr (not a row-count issue) — likely a rounding or source-data refresh difference
- **Auvik/Bitdefender future months**: Intentional cap at `DATE_TRUNC('MONTH', CURRENT_DATE())` in VENDOR_USAGE, but RECON_DETAIL CTEs read directly from vendor tables without this cap

**Outcome flags**: SentinelOne already uses single-token flags (CLEAR, MINOR_DRIFT, etc). ESET/Auvik/Bitdefender/Exium use compound `base_outcome_flag|MAT_...|SRC_...|OVR_...` format — the combined CTEs correctly apply `COALESCE(base_outcome_flag, SPLIT_PART)` for those but Proofpoint/SentinelOne/Webroot/Acronis/KeepIT pass through directly (which is fine since their flags are already single-token).
