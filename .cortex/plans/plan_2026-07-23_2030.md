The exact doubling (Nemsys: vendor=1482, zuora=2964) happens because billing rows for MULTIPLE products (CU-PP-ESS-PRO-PLUS AND CU-PP-ESS-PSAT-A) are matching against a single vendor row. Root cause is likely:
