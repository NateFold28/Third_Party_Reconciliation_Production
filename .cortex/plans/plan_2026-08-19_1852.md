## Context

**Key findings:**
1. Auvik is currently at 47-58% clear rate (improving over months as partner maps grow)
2. The main issues are:
   - **SKU mismatch** (37 rows/month): PERFORMANCE products on vendor side have no dedicated billing SKU in Zuora — Zuora bundles them into ESSENTIALS package SKUs (CULCSAS*, CWANNMM*)
   - **VENDOR_OVER_BILLING** (59 rows): Vendor usage qty exceeds Zuora billing qty at the product-group level, but at the account level they match better
   - **NO_BILLING_NO_HISTORY** (34 rows): Vendor-only accounts
3. The root cause: reconciling at `(sf_id, month, sku_match_group)` splits Performance from Essentials, but Zuora bills them as a single package. The manual team reconciles at the **account-month** level.
4. Auvik has two vendor streams (CW and CMS) that represent different billing entities but NO partner overlap — they're complementary, not duplicated.
5. No TRT API data exists for Auvik.
6. Partner mapping is excellent (297/299 mapped).

**Architecture decision:** Like Bitdefender, reconcile at `(sf_id, billing_month)` grain to match the manual team process. Show the product breakdown in VENDOR_PRODUCT but compare totals at the account level. This will significantly improve clear rate by eliminating false SKU_MISMATCH flags.
