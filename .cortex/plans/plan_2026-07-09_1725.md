Your existing renewal system operates at the **contract level** (CONTRACT_ID_UFR). The Product Loss Analysis needs to operate at the **product group/portfolio level** to understand *which products* are being lost, why, and what signals predict it.

### 2. `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`
Document all 8+ source tables with full column listings, freshness, grain, and join keys. Categorize by: Spine, Retention Metrics, Behavioral Signals, Firmographic, Billing/Financial.

### 3. `FEATURE_CATALOG.md`
Define ~60-80 features across these domains:
- **Product tenure & lifecycle** (product age, time since activation, contract length)
- **MRR trajectory** (MoM/YoY growth, max MRR vs current, seat count trends)
- **Multi-product context** (products on account, cross-sell depth, product mix)
- **Support/engagement** (cases by product, CSAT by product, CSM activity)
- **Billing health** (DPD, failed payments, credit memos, open balance)
- **Usage signals** (active members, tickets, devices managed, usage trend)
- **Prior loss history** (previous product losses on the account, churn pattern)
- **Renewal structural** (uplift rate, touch tier, region, segment)

### 4. `PRODUCT_LOSS_FEATURE_STORE.sql`
A SQL stored procedure that builds a product-level feature store with:
- **Grain:** `(ACCOUNT_ID, PRODUCT_GROUP_UFR, RENEWAL_MONTH, AS_OF_DATE)`
- **Spine:** CARR portfolio-level snap joined with product details
- **Point-in-time design:** Same architecture as your V5 (AS_OF_DATE prevents leakage)
- **Targets:** Product-level churn (binary), dollar loss amount, downsell indicator

### 5. `ANALYSIS_FRAMEWORK.md`
Goals, KPIs, product groups of interest, and how this differs from contract-level prediction.
